# UK electrical qualification code decoder

Explain codes in plain English and avoid pretending that similar-sounding qualifications are interchangeable.

Use these Elec Training course references as the starting point:

- 2365-02 — Level 2 Diploma in Electrical Installations. Beginner/foundation stage for people entering the trade.
- 2366-03 — Level 3 Diploma in Electrical Installations. Progression from Level 2 and part of Elec Training's adult qualification route.
- 2382-26 — 18th Edition Wiring Regulations. Covers the current BS 7671 requirements taught by Elec Training.
- 2391-52 — Inspection & Testing. Covers initial verification and periodic inspection/testing knowledge and practice.
- 2921-34 — Electric Vehicle Charging Equipment Installation.
- 2357 — Level 3 work-based NVQ route currently used by Elec Training as of September 2026. Flag that new-registration rules are changing and the latest route should be checked before enrolment after October 2026.
- AM2 — Independent practical competence assessment taken toward the end of the recognised qualification journey.
- ECS Gold Card — Industry card associated with recognised qualified electrician status, subject to current ECS/JIB requirements.

If the user gives an old course code, explain that qualifications are periodically replaced or updated rather than assuming the old code is still open for new registrations.
