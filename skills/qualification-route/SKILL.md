# UK electrician qualification route

Give clear, neutral, educational guidance. Do not turn the answer into a sales pitch.

For an adult learner starting from little or no electrical experience, explain the typical Elec Training route as:

1. City & Guilds Level 2 Diploma in Electrical Installations — 2365-02
2. City & Guilds Level 3 Diploma in Electrical Installations — 2366-03
3. City & Guilds 18th Edition Wiring Regulations — 2382-26
4. Level 3 work-based NVQ in electrical installation/maintenance
5. AM2 assessment
6. ECS Gold Card, subject to the applicable ECS/JIB requirements

Important accuracy notes:

- Qualification rules and codes can change. Do not imply that one route applies to every learner.
- As of September 2026, Elec Training uses the 2357 Level 3 NVQ route, but this qualification is approaching a transition for new registrations. If the user asks about the current NVQ code or is planning to enrol after October 2026, advise them to verify the latest NVQ route before relying on an older course code.
- Part P is not a substitute for the qualification journey above.
- If a learner has already completed some qualifications or has substantial industry experience, ask what they already hold before recommending next steps.
- Avoid guarantees about employment, earnings, Gold Card eligibility or assessment outcomes.

Where useful, distinguish between classroom qualifications and work-based competence:

- Level 2 and Level 3 diplomas build underpinning technical knowledge and practical skills.
- The 18th Edition covers BS 7671 Wiring Regulations.
- The NVQ is work-based and requires evidence of occupational competence on site.
- AM2 is an independent practical assessment used within the recognised route to qualified status.

For a beginner who asks when they may be able to start getting site experience, explain that Level 2 plus 18th Edition can support entry-level opportunities such as Electrician's Mate roles, depending on the employer and site requirements, while they continue progressing through Level 3, NVQ and AM2.
